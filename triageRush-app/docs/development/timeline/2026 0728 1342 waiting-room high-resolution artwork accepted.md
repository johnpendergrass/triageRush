## Approved visual direction

- The warm waiting-room ambiance and varied green/teal horizontal trim are
  retained; this trim is intentionally not matched to the rooms-panel wall.
- The floor/wall junction is slightly higher than in the former small artwork,
  providing more floor and making the separately layered patient and chair
  appear naturally grounded.
- Furniture, plants, tables, magazines, water dispensers, and other details are
  arranged around a useful open central area for the patient layer.
- Specialty and informational signs are generally in an upper corner, with
  left- or right-side placement selected to balance the furniture in each
  scene.

## Superseded artwork

The user accepted the high-resolution candidates, removed `-candidate` from
their filenames, and deleted the former small waiting-room backgrounds. The
small versions should not be restored as active assets. A separate copy of the
pre-redesign asset tree exists in the user's
`triageRush-app/assets - before size redo/` backup.
