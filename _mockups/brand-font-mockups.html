Brand font mockups — matching the ER ENTRANCE sign

Goal: a font family + color close to the "Triage RUSH!" channel letters in the entrance art, done entirely with CSS. Each card shows the treatment on a wall-gray stage, on the dark game-header gradient, and then at the brand's REAL size (17px) in a copy of the game header strip. Shadows are in em units, so what works big works small too.

Font stack everywhere: "Arial Black", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif at weight 900 — the sign's heavy grotesque, using fonts every device already has. "RUSH!" is 1.18× larger, matching the sign's proportions. If none of these feel right at 17px, the fallback plan is to reserve the treatment for larger text (review headers, ABOUT) and keep the game header simpler.
