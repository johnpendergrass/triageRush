### Queue and RUSH timing

- RUSH now renders at least five background-backed waiting slots, begins with
  two occupied slots, and grows through a hard maximum of ten.
- The 60-second arrival curve is 10, 9, 8, and so on to a one-second floor.
- The 120-second curve is 14.5, 13.5, 12.5, and so on through 1.5 to a
  one-second floor, reached with about eight seconds remaining.
- A synchronized 250 ms heartbeat drives both the whole-second shift clock and
  fractional patient intervals.
- Arrival pulses continue when the queue is full. They do not add an eleventh
  patient, but still play the arrival cue and trigger the full-queue effect.
- A blocked full-queue arrival briefly shakes the entire waiting panel in a
  quick horizontal left/right pattern.

### Sound and final countdown

- Added a reusable Web Audio context initialized from the Start Shift gesture.
- Added a checked-by-default RUSH clock-and-arrival-sounds setting.
- Added a light-medium clock tick at shift start and each whole second.
- Replaced the provisional two-note arrival sound with a bright single ding
  using a quieter upper harmonic.
- During the final five seconds, the clock clicks on the second, quarter
  second, and half second, then rests on the three-quarter second.
- At zero, a complementary lower dong plays; a coincident arrival ding is
  suppressed.
- During the final ten seconds, white numerals `10` through `1` pop into the
  upper third of the patient image and fade in place over about half a second.

### Scoring and header

- Removed the redundant TRIAGE/RUSH switch from the game header.
- Added a bordered numbers-only Correct / Close / Wrong = score card.
- Correct is green, Close yellow, Wrong red, and score white. Strict hides
  Close and its separator.
- Enlarged the timer and removed its border/background.
- Triage now scores Correct +100, Close +50, and Wrong -50, with no
  waiting-room penalty.
- RUSH uses the same assignment points and continuously subtracts 10 for every
  patient currently waiting. It therefore begins at -20.
- Final review uses the same derived score function, avoiding a second waiting
  deduction.

### Shift Review and Patients Seen

- Shift Review now shows formula rows for Correct, Close when enabled, Wrong,
  and Left Waiting, including count, point value, and subtotal.
- Under-triaged and Over-triaged counts moved into a separate Triage Direction
  section below scoring.
- Replaced the old per-patient arrow row with one `PATIENTS SEEN (n)` link.
- The link reuses the complete existing coach chart inside a conditional review
  wrapper; the coach content was not redesigned or duplicated.
- A fixed banner below the clipboard clamp supplies circular previous/next
  controls, patient index/name, and a dedicated close box.
- Closing returns to Shift Review and restores focus to the Patients Seen link.
