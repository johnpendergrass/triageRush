## Approved decisions

- The mobile game may grow beyond `430 x 764` in a desktop browser.
- The shell retains its 9:16 aspect ratio.
- Desktop top and bottom breathing room should each be approximately half the
  rendered height of one room door.
- The viewport-based sizing behavior means Chrome `+` and `-` zoom largely do
  not change the shell's physical size. John is considering that tradeoff; no
  alternative zoom behavior is approved yet.
- The automated door-sign result is not approved.
