RANDOM ASSIGNMENT
- Assign one of the sixteen backgrounds when a patient enters a waiting-room
  slot.
- Store the assigned background ID with that waiting-room appearance so it does
  not change on every render.
- Reassign or release the background when the patient leaves the waiting room.
- Repetition is acceptable, although the game may prefer unused backgrounds
  when several are available.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
- The background and patient are separate image layers.
- The complete waiting-room cell remains the touch target.
- Scale the whole fixed 360 x 640 game canvas uniformly; do not independently
  reflow or resize individual cells for different browser dimensions.

CREATION METHOD
- Built-in image generation created the sixteen visual variants.
- Each selected result was normalized locally to exactly 238 x 336 pixels.
- The wall/floor junction was enforced at raster y = 262 for every asset.
