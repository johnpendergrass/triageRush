### Current gameplay implemented

- Triage starts with five waiting patients and immediately refills the queue.
- Triage supports a five-minute shift or No Timer.
- TriageRUSH starts with two waiting patients and grows to a maximum of ten.
- The provisional RUSH arrival intervals are 10, 9, 8 seconds, and so on down
  to a one-second floor.
- RUSH supports 60- and 120-second shifts.
- Strict and Forgiving evaluation follow the required order in
  `docs/3-gameplay-rules-and-specs.md`.
- Ordinary patients receive full credit only for their matching ESI room.
- Psych and Discharge patients receive full credit for either the named special
  room or the underlying ESI room.
- Forgiving gives Close for an adjacent ESI room; Strict has no Close result.
- The provisional RUSH values are Correct +100, Close +50, Wrong -50, and -10
  for every patient left waiting.
- Close and Wrong feedback marks only the selected room. The correct room is
  not revealed.
- Queue patients move into an empty center panel or swap with an active,
  unassigned patient.
- A waiting-room background remains attached to its queued patient.
- Visible patient duplicates are prohibited through a shuffled patient deck.
- Assignment immediately clears the center and opens the selected room.
- Activating the open room recalls the same patient for another attempt.
- Only the first assignment affects shift statistics and RUSH score.
- Selecting another queued patient finalizes the previous assigned case.

### Charts, settings, and review

- The pre-assignment chart expands Presentation, locks Answer, and leaves
  Clinical available but collapsed.
- Coach unlocks after assignment and opens the same detailed chart with the
  answer and clinical explanation available.
- Chart content includes patient evidence, the player's choice, authoritative
  answer, destination rationale, resources, findings, red flags, teaching
  points, possible diagnoses, and possible disposition.
- Detailed charts pause the shift timer.
- Anchored Close and conditional `MORE ABOVE` / `MORE BELOW` controls remain.
- HOME now opens a compact settings view for:
  - player title and three uppercase initials;
  - Triage or RUSH;
  - Strict or Forgiving;
  - mode-appropriate shift length; and
  - UI hints.
- Player identity is persisted in local storage.
- Applying gameplay settings during an active shift requires restart
  confirmation.
- Shift Review reports identity, mode, difficulty, date/time, duration,
  patients seen, correct, close, over-triaged, under-triaged, wrong/special,
  patients waiting, and RUSH score when applicable.
- Seen patients can be reopened from Shift Review in Patient Review.
- The Shift Review footer control is the explicit End Shift control for No
  Timer and timed shifts.
