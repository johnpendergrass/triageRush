STATUS AND SCOPE
- This image is the selected visual authority for the seven CLOSED choices.
- It defines the appearance, ordering, dimensions, spacing, and surrounding
  wall treatment for ESI 1-5, Psych, and Discharge.
- The choices are gameplay treatment-assignment metaphors. They are presented
  as doors even though ESI levels are not literal hospital destinations.
- This sheet does not yet define the matching open-door artwork.
