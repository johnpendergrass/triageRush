Triage RUSH!

Triage RUSH! is a hospital ED triage game/simulator. With high-density UI and patient data, it will be a challenge for even the most ardent student of emergency medicine.

How to Play

  • Tap a waiting patient.
  • Tap the patient chart.
  • Tap a treatment door to move them to that room.
  • This is TRIAGE, not DIAGNOSIS!
  • Review your shift when done.
  • Triage! is a steady shift.
  • Triage RUSH! is faster paced.
  • ‘Strict’ requires the correct room.
  • ‘Forgiving’ allows ‘close enough’.

Patients, charts and artwork are fictional. A game, not medical training or advice.
